## 🏡 Now building: Matchwell

**Rental matchmaking that fits your life.**

Bedrooms and bathrooms are the starting point. Matchwell looks at the rest of the picture (light, quiet, work rhythm, transit, pets and the energy of the block) and matches renters to homes they'll actually love living in.

- 🧬 **Fit Fingerprint** scores every home against how you want to live
- 💬 **"Why this fits you"** explains each match in plain language, trade-offs included
- 🏢 **Owner portal** for landlords to list homes and review applications
- ✉️ **Built-in applications and messaging** between renters and owners

👉 **[See Matchwell live](https://tenant-rental-matchmaker.overskill.app)**
